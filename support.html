Support

Stuck on a lock, or found a bug? Email bycixix@gmail.com and include your device model and the job you were on.

How the controls work

Left thumb — the steady hand. Tension on a cylinder, pull on a padlock, pressure on a bolt, or a key's reach. Slide up into the band and hold.

Right thumb — the working hand. Pick, spin, lift, turn or push, depending on the lock.

I can't figure out a lock

  • Tap ? during a job for the tell: what the lock feels like and what to do about it.
  • Every new kind of lock starts with a short drill. You can replay drills from Field Notes (the ? on the job board).
  • The Practice Bench has the glass — an x-ray view of the lock. Use it to learn, then turn it off.
  • If something breaks or jams, the game warns you first: listen for grinding, and watch the strain gauge.

Progress

Progress is saved on your device automatically. Deleting the app deletes your progress. There are no accounts and nothing to restore.

Sound and haptics

Much of the feel is in the sound. Play with sound on (the speaker button, top right) and your device's silent switch off for the best experience. Haptics follow your system settings.
