Support

Stuck on a lock, or found a bug? Email bycixix@gmail.com and include your device model and the job you were on.

How the controls work

Left thumb — the steady hand. Tension on a cylinder, pull on a padlock, pressure on a bolt, or a key's reach. Slide up into the band and hold.

Right thumb — the working hand. Pick, spin, lift, turn or push, depending on the lock.

I can't figure out a lock

  • Tap ? during a job for the tell: what the lock feels like and what to do about it.
  • Every new kind of lock starts with a short drill. You can replay drills from Field Notes (the ? on the job board).
  • The Practice Bench has the glass — an x-ray view of the lock. Use it to learn, then turn it off.
  • If something breaks or jams, the game warns you first: listen for grinding, and watch the strain gauge.

Progress

Progress is saved on your device automatically. If you are signed in to iCloud, it is also kept in your iCloud account and follows you to a new iPhone: sign in with the same Apple Account, install LOCKSMITH, and your jobs, stars and records come back. Reset Progress in Settings starts over everywhere.

Tonight’s Lock and After Hours

Tonight’s Lock is a new lock every night, the same all evening. Open it on consecutive nights to build a streak. After Hours opens after five jobs: contract after contract on one kit of tools, until the heat from your marks ends the night.

Left-handed

Settings → Left-handed swaps the two pads, so your working hand is on the left. All the instructions swap with it.

Sound and haptics

Much of the feel is in the sound. Play with sound on (the speaker button, top right) and your device's silent switch off for the best experience. Haptics, including the continuous texture you feel while working a lock, can be turned off in Settings.
