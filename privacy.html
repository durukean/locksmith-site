Privacy policy

LOCKSMITH is a lockpicking puzzle game. It is built to work without knowing anything about you.

The short version

We collect nothing. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Nothing is ever sent to us.

Data stored on your device

Your progress — completed jobs, stars, earnings, your chosen tool, which lessons you have seen and your sound setting — is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

Apple services you can choose to use

These are provided by Apple, under Apple’s privacy policy. None of their data is sent to us.

  • iCloud. If you are signed in to iCloud, your progress is copied to your own iCloud account so it can follow you to a new iPhone. Only you can read it.
  • Game Center. If you sign in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are shared with Game Center, and your Game Center nickname appears on its leaderboards.
  • Nightly reminder. If you turn it on in Settings, your iPhone shows a local notification at 8 pm. It is scheduled on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, advertising, analytics or crash reporting. Apart from the Apple services above, it does not use the internet.

Children

LOCKSMITH does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if a future version adds a feature that needs the network), this page will be updated before that version is released.
